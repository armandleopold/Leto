# WireGuard logo

Shown beside a PC that Leto reaches through its own WireGuard tunnel.

- `wireguard.svg`: the WireGuard mark from Simple Icons 13.21.0
  (`icons/wireguard.svg`, CC0-1.0), on a white disc, in WireGuard's red
  `#88171A`.
- `wireguard.png`: the same, 96 × 96, which the app draws. Made from the SVG
  in headless Chromium.

"WireGuard" and the WireGuard logo are registered trademarks of Jason A.
Donenfeld. Leto only uses them to say that a connection goes through
WireGuard.
